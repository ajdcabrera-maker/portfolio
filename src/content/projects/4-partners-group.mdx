## Executive Summary

At Partners Group I design internal platforms for people who live in high-density financial data, multi-role permissions, and long approval chains. The job is clarity without dumping complexity on the user.

---

## Challenges & Focus Areas

* **Information Density:** Legacy screens packed with tables, unclear status, and scattered actions.
* **Role-Based Workflows:** Interfaces that change with role, permission, and approval state.
* **Task Efficiency:** Shorter multi-step admin flows, with fewer steps and fewer operational errors.

---

## Design Approach

* **Systems Thinking:** Break bottlenecks into modular UI patterns and clearer navigation.
* **Domain Alignment:** Sit with domain experts and engineers so backend states read clearly in the UI.
