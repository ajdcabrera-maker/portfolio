## Executive Summary

As Usability and Design Manager at Cebu Pacific Air, I led end-to-end UX/UI strategy across high-traffic digital booking platforms[cite: 1]. Serving millions of passengers worldwide[cite: 1], the initiative focused on unifying fragmented digital touchpoints, establishing robust Design Ops[cite: 1], and driving measurable business performance.

* **+15% User Satisfaction:** Streamlined core flight booking and check-in funnels, significantly reducing user friction[cite: 1].

* **Design Ops Infrastructure:** Created a unified single source of truth connecting Figma component libraries to front-end engineering via **Storybook** and **Zeroheight**[cite: 1].
