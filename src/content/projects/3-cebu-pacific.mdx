## Executive Summary

As Usability and Design Manager at Cebu Pacific Air, I led end-to-end UX/UI strategy across high-traffic digital booking platforms. Serving millions of passengers worldwide, the initiative focused on unifying fragmented digital touchpoints, establishing robust Design Ops, and driving measurable business performance.

---

## Key Achievements & Impact

* **+15% User Satisfaction:** Streamlined core flight booking and check-in funnels, significantly reducing user friction.
* **+3% Conversion Rate:** Optimized seat selection, baggage add-ons, and payment checkout flows through iterative usability testing and data-driven design.
* **Design Ops Infrastructure:** Created a unified single source of truth connecting Figma component libraries to front-end engineering via **Storybook** and **Zeroheight**.

---

## Strategy & Execution

* **Usability Testing & Data-Driven UX**: Conducted user testing sessions across desktop, mobile web, iOS/Android apps, and chatbot platforms. Insights were directly translated into UI optimizations and conversion funnel enhancements.
* **Enterprise Handoff & Governance**: Partnered closely with engineering, product management, and business stakeholders to align design execution with technical feasibility and business goals. Technical background informed component construction, reducing front-end layout drift.
