## Executive Summary

Most people struggle with portfolio layout, type hierarchy, and token consistency, not with writing their own bio.

Folio.md is an AI portfolio generator that takes an outdated CV plus a mood photo or URL, then outputs refined copy, a custom `DESIGN.md` theme, and deployable web code.

---

## Product Workflow

1. **User Input**: Outdated CV PDF, photo or web-link inspiration, and short Q&A.
2. **AI Engine Processing**: Parse the CV, fill career gaps through prompts, extract palette and type rules.
3. **Dynamic Output**: Refined copy, custom `DESIGN.md` theme, published site code.

## Core AI UX Patterns

* **Conversational Gap Analysis**: Fill career gaps with targeted Q&A instead of blank forms.
* **Zero-Jargon Visual Theme Extraction**: Pull palettes and fonts from photos, no hex codes required.
* **Real-Time Token Governance**: Generated components stay on system tokens; humans still control layout density.
